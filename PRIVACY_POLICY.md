# Privacy Policy for Togomo

**Last Updated: April 15, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Togomo ("Company," "we," "us," "our," or "Service") operates the Togomo food delivery aggregator platform ("App" or "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise process personal information in connection with our mobile application, website, and related services.

By accessing or using Togomo, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use our Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Registration:** Name, email address, phone number, password
- **Profile Information:** Delivery addresses, payment methods, preferences
- **Location Data:** GPS coordinates, address history
- **Payment Information:** Credit/debit card details (processed securely via third-party payment processors)
- **Communication:** Messages, reviews, ratings, feedback
- **Identity Verification:** Government-issued ID (for partner verification)

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information:** Device type, OS version, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data:** App interactions, order history, search queries, features accessed
- **Location Information:** Real-time GPS location (with your permission)
- **Log Data:** IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps, error logs
- **Cookies & Tracking:** Device identifiers for analytics and personalization

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Payment Processors:** Transaction details and verification status
- **Restaurant Partners:** Order updates and operational information
- **Delivery Partners:** Real-time location and delivery status
- **Advertising Networks:** User interaction data for targeted advertising

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- **Service Delivery:** Process orders, manage payments, coordinate deliveries
- **Communication:** Send order updates, promotional offers, support responses
- **Personalization:** Customize recommendations, improve user experience
- **Analytics:** Understand usage patterns, improve functionality
- **Safety & Security:** Prevent fraud, detect abuse, enforce policies
- **Legal Compliance:** Comply with regulations, respond to legal requests
- **Marketing:** Send newsletters (with opt-in), promotional content
- **Improvement:** Develop new features, optimize performance

## 4. Information Sharing

We may share your information with:

### 4.1 Service Providers
- Payment processors and financial institutions
- Cloud hosting and data storage providers
- Analytics platforms
- Customer support software
- Mapping and location services

### 4.2 Business Partners
- Restaurant partners (to fulfill orders)
- Delivery partners (location and order details)
- Marketing and advertising partners

### 4.3 Legal Requirements
- Law enforcement and government agencies (when required by law)
- To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property
- In connection with legal proceedings

### 4.4 Business Transfers
- In case of merger, acquisition, bankruptcy, or sale
- Personal information may be transferred as part of the transaction

**We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.**

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:
- Encrypted data transmission (TLS/SSL protocol)
- Password hashing and secure authentication
- Access controls and role-based permissions
- Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
- Secure payment processing (PCI DSS compliant)

**However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.** We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Account Information:** Retained while your account is active; deleted upon request (with exceptions for legal/tax purposes)
- **Transaction Records:** Retained for 7 years (tax/legal requirements)
- **Location Data:** Real-time data deleted after order completion; historical data deleted after 90 days
- **Cookies:** Remain until expiration or manual deletion

## 7. Your Rights & Choices

### 7.1 Access & Correction
- You can view and update your profile information in the app
- Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you

### 7.2 Deletion (Right to be Forgotten)
- Request deletion of your account and associated data
- Some data may be retained for legal, tax, or operational reasons

### 7.3 Marketing Communications
- Opt-out of promotional emails via unsubscribe link
- Disable push notifications in app settings
- Contact us to modify communication preferences

### 7.4 Location Data
- Enable/disable location access in device settings
- Clear location history in app settings
- Disable real-time tracking while retaining account

### 7.5 Cookie Management
- Delete cookies through browser settings
- Disable cookies (may affect app functionality)

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for users under 13 years old (or the age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the information and terminate the child's account.

## 9. International Data Transfers

If you access Togomo from outside India, your information may be transferred to, processed in, or stored in countries with different data protection laws. By using our Service, you consent to such transfers.

## 10. Third-Party Links & Services

Our App may contain links to third-party websites and services (restaurants, payment processors, etc.). This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party services, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies before providing personal information.
